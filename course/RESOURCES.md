# Course resources

## Knowledge

All sources for the course are in the repository bibliography, which publishes only sources verified at source:

- [`../bibliography/annotated-bibliography.md`](../bibliography/annotated-bibliography.md), grouped into seven sections, with a "where to start" list
- [`../bibliography/errata.md`](../bibliography/errata.md), known errors in published CA reference lists

Primary source for lessons 1 to 4: Mayne, J. (2019). Revisiting contribution analysis. *Canadian Journal of Program Evaluation*, 34(2), 171 to 191. https://doi.org/10.3138/cjpe.68004 (open access).

## Wisdom (communities)

- Uganda Evaluation Association and the annual Uganda Evaluation Week, hosted with the Office of the Prime Minister. Use for testing CA designs on local evaluations. https://opm.go.ug/uganda-evaluation-week-2024/
- BetterEvaluation's Causal Pathways resources, including an introductory session on CA. Use for practitioner discussion of CA in complex settings. https://www.betterevaluation.org/methods-approaches/themes/causal-pathways

## Gaps

- Mayne (2011), the *Evaluating the complex* chapter, is not yet located in full text.
- Few published CAs from Africa: Noltze et al. (2014) and Anguko (2019) are nearly all there is.
