# Contribution Analysis glossary

Canonical terms for this course. A term enters only once the learner uses it correctly in practice.

## Questions

**Contribution question**:
An evaluation question about whether, and how, the intervention contributed to a specified result.
_Avoid_: effectiveness question, attribution question

**Causes-of-outcome question**:
An evaluation question about what brought about an observed outcome, with the intervention as one candidate cause.
_Avoid_: impact question

## Factors

**Other factor**:
Any causal factor beyond the intervention that may bear on the result. Neutral term; classify before labelling.

**Support factor**:
A factor inside the causal package that must hold for the intervention to work; captured in the ToC as a causal link assumption (Mayne 2019).
_Avoid_: rival (it works with the intervention, not against it)

**External factor**:
A causal factor outside the intervention's package. It may be linked to the intervention's pathway or not. Mayne (2019, p. 175) says external factors are usually other causal factors at work, not alternative or rival explanations.

**Rival explanation**:
An explanation that, if it holds, would account for the result without the intervention. Mayne (2019, p. 175) calls the "alternative" and "rival" terms inappropriate in the context of complex causality; check where a factor sits before using the label.
