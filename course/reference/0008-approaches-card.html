Approaches card

The essentials from Lesson 8.

Four types of causality (Palenberg 2023)

  • Counterfactual: compare with a world without the intervention
  • Generative: chains of cause and effect; CA's position from about 2012 (p. 397)
  • INUS: basis of contributory causes and packages (p. 395)
  • Probabilistic: elements integrated into CA

CA and process tracing

A combined approach to generative inference; tests judge evidence strength; the aim is confidence about impact (Befani and Mayne 2014). The "how" part of a claim still needs the causal narrative (Mayne 2019, p. 182).

The family (Leeuw 2023)

  • CA: did it contribute, and how; mainly constructive (p. 415)
  • Realist evaluation: what works, for whom, in what circumstances
  • Policy-scientific: test, challenge and criticise the theory (p. 415)

Choose for the question

  • Contribution and how: CA plus tests
  • For whom: realist, or CA with nested ToCs
  • Assumptions: policy-scientific
  • How much versus none: counterfactual design, with CA to explain
