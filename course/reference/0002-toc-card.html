ToC card

The essentials from Lesson 2. Page numbers refer to Mayne (2019), CJPE 34(2), 171 to 191.

Terms (p. 172)

  • Impact pathway: steps from activities to impact
  • Theory of change: pathway plus the causal assumptions behind each link
  • Causal link assumption: an event or condition necessary, or likely necessary, for a link to work
  • Results: outputs, outcomes, impacts

Can this ToC carry a claim?

  • Every link has causal link assumptions (p. 176)
  • Results and terms are defined clearly enough to confirm (pp. 176 to 177)
  • Assumptions are necessary or likely necessary (p. 177)
  • There is a timeline (p. 183)
  • Key mechanisms are backed by prior research (p. 177)
  • It is built to be evaluated, not just to fund or communicate (p. 178)
  • Complex interventions are unpacked into nested ToCs (p. 178)

No assumptions, no credible CA claim (p. 176).

COM-B pathway (pp. 178 to 179)

  1. Activities and outputs
  2. Reach and reaction
  3. Capacity change: capability, opportunity, motivation
  4. Behaviour change
  5. Direct benefits
  6. Improved wellbeing

Assumptions on every arrow; external influences alongside.

Three levels (p. 178)

  • Narrative: brief account, for communication
  • Overview: pathways and rationale, usually no link assumptions
  • Nested: per pathway, place or actor group, with explicit assumptions. The level CA tests

Build nested before simplified (pp. 180 to 181).

Partly met assumptions (p. 177)

Less of the result, but still necessary. Define results precisely so they line up with assumptions.
