The essentials from Lesson 6.

The cycle (Apgar and Levine 2024)

  1. Set out the attribution problem
  2. Develop the ToC and risks
  3. Gather existing evidence
  4. Assemble and assess the story
  5. Seek additional evidence
  6. Revise and strengthen; feed back to 4 and 2

Four lessons (Hopkins 2021)

  • Start at the end
  • Accommodate uncertainty
  • Understand what is "enough"
  • Bring people along

Expect (Buetti et al. 2025)

  • Time and resource demands
  • Hard judgements on evidence levels and which external factors to include
  • Stakeholder turnover and slow data access
  • ToCs often built retrospectively

Check credibility

Steering group review of working documents, researcher review of a case study, and a reviewer of key findings (Congo Basin example, Apgar and Levine 2024).
