Other factors card

The essentials from Lesson 4.

Mayne's rule (2019, pp. 175 to 176)

  • Contribution question: outside factors need not play a major role, unless causally linked to the package
  • Causes-of-outcome question: explore them
  • External factors are usually other causal factors, not rivals

Other views

  • Lemire et al. (2012): the Relevant Explanation Finder for weighing factors
  • Budhwani and McDavid (2017, pp. 4, 12): alternatives stand in for counterfactuals; search for them before data collection
  • Ton et al. (2019, p. 6): no evaluation can test every alternative

Inside or outside the package

  • Inside: a support factor; verify it as a causal link assumption
  • Outside, linked: examine it
  • Outside, not linked: examine it only for a causes-of-outcome question

Strength of evidence

Only doubly decisive evidence eliminates a rival hypothesis; a passed smoking gun substantially weakens it (Collier 2011, p. 825). Say how strong the evidence on each factor is.
