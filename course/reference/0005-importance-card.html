Importance card

The essentials from Lesson 5.

What CA cannot do alone

Estimate size or relative importance (Mayne 2019, p. 181), or how much of an outcome is due to the intervention (p. 187). Package members are equal (Mayne 2015, pp. 128 to 129).

Four perspectives (p. 181; Palenberg 2023, pp. 394 to 395)

  1. Perceived importance
  2. Roles played
  3. Funds expended
  4. Extent of the constraints to change

Define the lens before you compare.

Stretching CA (Ton et al. 2019)

  • Build a hierarchy or ranking (pp. 5, 9)
  • Update confidence step by step (p. 8)
  • Interlink statistical, survey and case evidence (p. 9)
  • Use detailed ToCs and system maps (p. 10)
  • Report ranges, not points (p. 10)

Cautions

  • Factors that only work in combination cannot be sized one by one (p. 9)
  • Interlinked interventions: both or neither can claim the full effect (p. 7)
