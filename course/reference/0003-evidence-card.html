Evidence card

The essentials from Lesson 3.

Two parts, two jobs (Mayne 2019, p. 182)

  • Necessity: the intervention was an essential package member, so it contributed
  • Sufficiency: the package was enough, which explains how

Showing the ToC happened is not enough; add a causal narrative (pp. 182 to 183).

Table 1 tools (p. 184)

  1. Verify results and assumptions
  2. Hoop: time sequence
  3. Hoop: reasonable effort
  4. Hoop: expected effects
  5. Sufficiency: causal narrative
  6. Smoking gun: unique effects

Failing a hoop makes causality very unlikely; passing does not confirm (p. 183).

Process tracing tests (Collier 2011, p. 825)

  • Straw-in-the-wind: neither necessary nor sufficient
  • Hoop: necessary, not sufficient. Fail eliminates
  • Smoking gun: sufficient, not necessary. Pass confirms
  • Doubly decisive: both. Pass confirms and eliminates rivals

Confidence

Ask how likely the evidence is if the claim is true, and if it is false. Evidence expected either way adds little (Befani and Mayne 2014; Ton et al. 2019, p. 8).
