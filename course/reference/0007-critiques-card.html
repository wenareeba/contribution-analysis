Contribution analysis, reference card 7 of 8

Critiques card

The essentials from Lesson 7.

Critiques (Budhwani and McDavid 2017)

  • No standards (p. 8)
  • Positive bias (p. 9)
  • Vague "reasonable person" test (pp. 9 to 10)
  • Subjective evidence judgements (p. 10)
  • Resource intensive (pp. 10 to 11)

Other critiques

  • Claims rest on opinion; ToCs hide mechanisms (Schmitt and Beach 2015)
  • A label of rigour, not practice (Delahais and Toulemonde 2017, p. 371)
  • Linear causality; roles lumped together (Sridharan et al. 2023, pp. 376 to 381)
  • Less precise and testable (Leeuw 2023, p. 414)
  • Improving the narrative, not the policy (p. 415)

Mayne's responses (2019)

  • Opinion is not the basis of claims (p. 175)
  • A confirmed ToC greatly reduces bias (p. 177)
  • Nested ToCs unpack complexity (p. 178)
  • Causal link assumptions capture context and mechanisms (p. 185)
  • How far to explore other factors depends on the question (pp. 175 to 176)

Do this

  • Set thresholds before looking
  • Seek disconfirming evidence
  • Test "it made the difference" claims, do not count them
  • Explain mechanisms
  • Report methods fully
