## Reading the results

- Hoop tests keep a claim alive; failing one makes causality unlikely, passing one does not confirm it (p. 183).
- A unique effect is strong evidence that the intervention was a causal factor, but says nothing about the rest of the package (p. 186).
- The sufficiency narrative is what supplies part 2 of the claim (pp. 182, 185).
